# Slepé porovnanie modelov — 3 sady po 10 otázok

Rovnaké zadanie, rovnaká téma, líši sa len model, ktorý otázky napísal. Prečítaj si sady a povedz: **ktorá sada je najlepšia** (a prípadne poradie), plus čísla otázok, ktoré sa ti zvlášť páčia alebo nepáčia (napr. B4).


## Sada A

| # | Otázka | Odpoveď | Obtiažnosť |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Who is the only person ever to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences? | Marie Curie | easy |
| A2 | How many hearts does an octopus have? | Three | easy |
| A3 | Counting its overseas territories, which country spans the most time zones, beating both Russia and the USA? | France | hard |
| A4 | Which is the only country whose national flag is not a rectangle? | Nepal | easy |
| A5 | Scotland's official national animal doesn't actually exist. What is it? | Unicorn | medium |
| A6 | On hot summer days, the Eiffel Tower can grow up to about 15 centimetres taller. Why? | Thermal expansion | easy |
| A7 | The Caesar salad has nothing to do with Rome. In which country was it actually invented? | Mexico | medium |
| A8 | Which lake, the deepest on Earth at more than 1,600 metres, holds about a fifth of the world's unfrozen surface fresh water? | Lake Baikal | hard |
| A9 | The marathon's oddly precise 42.195 km was fixed so the race could finish in front of the royal box. In which city were those 1908 Olympics held? | London | medium |
| A10 | Which popular nut grows dangling from the bottom of a fleshy fruit called an 'apple'? | Cashew | medium |
