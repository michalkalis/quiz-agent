Trubbo is a voice-first trivia quiz built for the road. Start a quiz, listen to the question, and answer out loud. No tapping, no reading, just talking.

WHY TRUBBO
• Fully voice controlled: start, answer, skip and repeat with your voice
• Questions are read aloud with natural speech
• Answer in your own words. AI understands what you mean, not just exact matches
• Plays in Slovak and English
• Built for driving: audio-first flow, big controls, screen stays awake

FREE TO PLAY
You get a monthly allowance of free questions. The Unlimited subscription removes the limit, and one-time question packs never expire. Prices are shown in the app before any purchase.

STAY SAFE
Trubbo is designed for hands-free use. Never operate the screen while driving and always follow traffic laws.

GOOD TO KNOW
Questions and answer evaluation are AI generated and may contain inaccuracies. You can export or delete your data anytime in Settings.
