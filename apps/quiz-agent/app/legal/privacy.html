Aké údaje spracúvame

  • Nákupy: stav predplatného a balíčkov. Platby spracúva výhradne Apple; k číslu vašej karty nemáme prístup.
  • Diagnostika: záznamy o pádoch a chybách aplikácie (Sentry), aby sme vedeli opravovať problémy.
  • Štatistika používania: udalosti v aplikácii (napríklad spustený kvíz alebo zobrazená ponuka predplatného), ktoré ukladáme vo vlastnej databáze bez reklamných identifikátorov, aby sme vedeli aplikáciu zlepšovať.
  • Vlastné balíky a spätná väzba: téma, ktorú zadáte pri objednávke balíka, a spätná väzba, ktorú nám pošlete (text, snímka obrazovky, prípadne hlasová správa).

Nezobrazujeme reklamu a nesledujeme vás naprieč aplikáciami iných spoločností.

Privacy Policy · Trubbo

Effective: October 7, 2026

Who we are

Trubbo (a voice-first trivia quiz app) is operated by missinghue s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 07288093, with its registered office at Kotěrova 1588/2a, Dejvice, 160 00 Prague 6, Czech Republic ("we"). We are the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@missinghue.com.

What we process

  • Account & identifiers: an anonymous device identifier and sign-in tokens created on first launch. If you optionally sign in with Apple, we also process what Apple shares with us (name, e-mail or its private relay address).
  • Voice: recordings of your answers and voice commands are processed solely to convert speech to text. Answer recordings are not stored permanently; we store the text of your answer as part of your quiz history. If you submit voice feedback, we keep that recording until the feedback is handled.
  • Quiz activity: answers, scores, monthly usage counts (for the free-tier limit), question ratings.
  • Purchases: subscription and pack status. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports (Sentry) so we can fix problems.
  • Usage statistics: in-app events (for example a started quiz or a viewed subscription offer), stored in our own database without advertising identifiers, so we can improve the app.
  • Custom packs and feedback: the topic you enter when ordering a pack, and the feedback you send us (text, screenshot and, if you choose, a voice message).

We show no ads and do not track you across other companies' apps.

Artificial intelligence

Trubbo is an AI-powered app: quiz questions are AI-generated and your spoken answers are evaluated by AI. Content may therefore contain inaccuracies. You are interacting with an automated system, not a human.

Legal bases and purposes

  • Contract: providing the quiz, evaluating answers, managing subscriptions and limits.
  • Legitimate interest: diagnostics, usage statistics, security, abuse prevention.
  • Consent: microphone access (granted via the iOS system dialog, revocable any time in Settings).

Recipients (processors)

We rely on these providers: Apple (distribution, payments, sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription management), ElevenLabs (speech-to-text), OpenAI and Anthropic/OpenRouter (processing answer text, generating and evaluating quiz content, speech synthesis), Sentry (error diagnostics), Fly.io (hosting; EU servers in Paris). Data is shared only to the extent necessary for each purpose.

Retention and deletion

Account data and quiz history are kept while you use the app. You can delete your data at any time directly in the app (Settings → Delete my data) or by e-mail. After deletion, your personal data is removed without undue delay. Purchase records are kept for as long as accounting rules and refund handling require. Your answers and question ratings are kept only in anonymised form for statistics, and pack orders are kept without the link to your account and without the topic you entered.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Exercise them via hello@missinghue.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz) or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

Children

The app is not directed at children without adult supervision; the App Store age rating applies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version will always be available at this address with its effective date.
