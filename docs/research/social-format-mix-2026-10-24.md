# Instagram format mix for a 0-follower discovery account (2026-10-24)

Scope: faceless trivia brand Trubbo, two accounts (EN @trubboapp, SK/CZ), reels cross-posted to YouTube Shorts. Goal: reach and follows from non-followers.
Evidence grades: **[P]** platform/official (Instagram, Meta, Mosseri) · **[I]** industry dataset or vendor blog · **[A]** anecdotal/inferred. Where no data exists it says so.

## 1. TL;DR recommendation

**Per account, per week: 5 Reels + 2 carousels + Stories only as a cheap by-product. No single-image posts.**

- Ratio of feed posts about 70% Reels / 30% carousels. Reels are the discovery engine, carousels are the second distribution path plus the save/send format. Single images: skip (lowest reach and saves in every dataset).
- Cadence: 7 feed posts/week sits in Buffer's 6-9/week tier. Returns diminish above that, so do not add volume before quality. Floor: 3 posts/week (never zero weeks, there is a measured "no-posting penalty").
- Stories: 0-3 per week for now, zero extra production (re-share the week's best Reel; no quiz/poll stickers: the API cannot add them, founder 2026-10-22). Stories do not reach non-followers; they matter only once people follow. Do not spend design time on them yet.
- Trial Reels: NOT available at 0 followers (needs 200 for a professional account, see 3.4). Plan to switch them on at 200 followers and use them to test hooks.
- Cross-posting the same clean Reel to YouTube Shorts is fine: no cross-platform penalty, only watermarks hurt. Post natively, no logo of the other platform, vary caption.
- Make every Reel and every carousel built for **sends** (the top unconnected-reach signal): "send this to the friend who always thinks they know it".
- Run it as an experiment: after 4 weeks compare median reach per Reel vs per carousel in Insights on our own accounts, then shift the ratio (section 4). Public data conflicts on small accounts (3.2).

## 2. What each format does for discovery

| Format | Reaches non-followers? | Best signal | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | Yes, the main route (Reels tab, Explore, feed recs). Most datasets: highest non-follower reach for small accounts | Watch time (avg % watched, replays), then sends per reach [P] | Core of the week for a 0-follower account; the only format that also feeds YouTube Shorts |
| Carousels | Yes. Eligible for recommendations; Mosseri: get a "second chance" (re-shown from slide 2 if not swiped) [P] | Swipes (dwell time), saves, sends | 1-2 per week: answer-reveal quizzes, "can you get 5/5" sets; best data for saves |
| Single image | Weakest. Recommendable but lowest reach/saves/views in datasets [I] | Likes, sends | Skip while building; maybe an occasional "question of the day" later |
| Stories | No (shown to followers only) [P] | Replies, taps, stickers | Retention of existing followers; fewer unfollows for accounts that post them [P/A]. Useless for discovery at 0 followers |
| Trial Reels | Yes, by design: shown to non-followers first, hidden from followers [P] | Views, shares, watch time in first 24-72h | Hook/format testing once eligible (200+ followers) |

## 3. Evidence by topic

### 3.1 What Instagram distributes to non-followers
- Mosseri (Jan 2025): top three ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; "likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content". [P] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/
- Mosseri (Oct 2024) on carousels: more media means more interactions and more reach; if someone doesn't swipe, Instagram "will often give that carousel a second chance" starting at the second slide. Recommends carousels (up to 20 slides, music). [P] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/ig-chief-recommends-posting-carousels-to-improve-reach/730232/
- Mosseri on surfaces: Reels and carousels in Feed can reach new people; "Stories are awesome, but your average creator reaches way more people in Feed". [P, as quoted in secondary coverage] https://houseofmarketers.com/instagram-chief-profile-growth-tips-2025/
- Originality: accounts that mostly re-upload others' content are not recommended to non-followers. Applied to Reels since 2024, extended to photos and carousels on 2026-04-30. Watermarked content and reposts are ineligible for recommendations. [P/I] https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/ · https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232
- Caution for us: templated, faceless quiz content is original (our own questions, our own design), but keep each post visibly distinct (different question, visuals, voice) so it never reads as bulk template output. [A]

### 3.2 Large-sample data: reach and engagement per format
Datasets disagree on small accounts. Reason: different metrics (reach per follower vs views per post vs engagement rate) and different populations (influencers vs brands). Treat as "Reels most likely, carousels close behind", not as a proven ratio.
- Fanpage Karma, >3M posts, Jan-Apr 2026: accounts under 10K followers, median reach per follower Reels 4.9%, images 3.5%, carousels 2.6%. Reels reach about 3x as many non-followers as carousels for small accounts. Authors caveat it cannot prove format causes the difference. [I] https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-format-reach/
- Metricool/HypeAuditor, 700M posts, Jan-Jun 2025 (via secondary summaries): accounts under 1,000 followers, median reach Reels 134, carousels 56, images 23. Reels drive reach at every size; carousels beat single images on every metric (about 9x saves, 2x reach). [I] https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/ (press release; the per-tier numbers come from the secondary summary https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/etude-instagram-formats-leviers-fonctionnent-2026/ and are unverified against the primary report)
- Metricool, 391K business accounts: Reels 11% of posts but 37% of reach; Stories 72% of posts at about 3% reach. [I] https://www.prdaily.com/by-the-numbers-the-most-popular-formats-times-to-post-on-instagram/
- Socialinsider 2026 benchmark: engagement rate carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%. **Counter-evidence:** average views per post for 1-5K accounts: carousels 993, Reels 580, images 417 (saves 1/1/1, comments Reels 3, carousels 2). An older Socialinsider edition said Reels bring more impressions for accounts under 50K. [I] https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks
- Dash Social 2026 (brands, secondary summary): Reels reach 1.36x carousels and 2.25x single images; another summary claims photos/carousels out-reached Reels for brands, i.e. unresolved. Primary PDF too large to fetch. [I] https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2026/H1/instagram.pdf
- Not found: any dataset isolating accounts with ~0 followers, or non-follower reach by format from Buffer or Hootsuite or Later. Hootsuite "2-3x reach of images" claim seen only in a third-party blog, not verified.

### 3.3 Posting frequency
- Buffer, 2.1M posts from 102K accounts (feed posts only, formats not separated): vs 1-2 posts/week, 3-5/week gives about 12% more reach per post and +0.26% vs +0.12% weekly follower growth; 6-9/week +18% reach, +0.44%; 10+/week +24%, +0.66%. Zero-post weeks hurt growth ("no-posting penalty"). Buffer: 3-5/week is the sweet spot, more is diminishing returns. [I] https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/
- Metricool/HypeAuditor: 2-3 posts/week about 19% growth, 10+/week about 79%; says consistency matters more than volume. [I] https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/
- Socialinsider: median account posts about 8 Reels, 5 carousels, 7 images per month (2025). We would be well above median. [I] same URL as 3.2.
- Correlation, not causation: bigger accounts also post more. Metricool 2026: posts with hashtags got 32% fewer views (correlation). [I] https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-views-up-27-percent-in-early-2026-as-publishing-increases-report-finds/

### 3.4 Stories for a 0-follower account
- Buffer excluded Stories because they are "not typically shown to non-followers... not a game-changer for growing your account". [I] (Buffer link above)
- Mosseri: Stories will not help reach non-followers; they retain followers (fewer unfollows when posting Stories). [P/A, secondary coverage] https://houseofmarketers.com/instagram-chief-profile-growth-tips-2025/
- Conclusion: at 0 followers Stories have no audience. Value starts once there are followers to retain and ask (polls, quiz stickers). No dataset found on Stories sticker impact for small trivia accounts.

### 3.5 Trial Reels
- Launched 2024-12-10. Toggle "Trial" before sharing; shown to non-followers only, hidden from profile grid, followers' feed and Reels tab. Metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) available about 24h after; optional auto-share to followers if it performs well in the first 72h. Scheduling added Feb 2026 (secondary). [P] https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first/ · https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels
- Eligibility: public account eligible for recommendations; **professional account 200+ followers, personal account 1,000+**. Seen in Help Center summaries; I could not load the Help Center page itself, and many creator blogs still say 1,000. Verify in the app toggle. [P via secondary] https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-trial-reels
- Reels only. Mosseri (Aug 2026 Q&A, secondary): trial posts for photos and carousels not available, "we really should do that". [P via secondary] https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-trial-posts-photos-carousels/
- Our situation: at ~0 followers we cannot use it. Use it from 200 followers to test the first 3 seconds (hook) and question types. Success signals to read: watch time above our average, sends from cold viewers, profile visits. [I] https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-guide/

### 3.6 Cross-posting to YouTube Shorts
- No platform detects or penalises the same video on another platform; the penalties are visible watermarks/low-res recycled clips (Instagram recommendation guidelines) and aggregator behaviour. YouTube's Oct 2026 Shorts update reduces reach for channels re-uploading **other creators'** clips, not creators' own content. [P/I] https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232 · https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/ · https://www.post-bridge.com/blog/does-cross-posting-hurt-engagement
- YouTube "inauthentic content" (July 2025) targets mass-produced, near-identical template videos for monetisation. Our trivia template is a mild risk for monetisation, not for reach; keep content varied. [P/I] https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-clarifies-july-15-policy-update-repetitious-content-rebranded-as-inauthentic-content/
- Upload native files (export without any Instagram or TikTok overlay), separate captions/titles, separate YouTube channel per language. Whether a reel's sends signal on Instagram is affected by Shorts: no evidence.

### 3.7 Trivia/quiz-specific notes
- No dataset found for trivia accounts specifically. Everything below is inference. [A]
- Carousels: question on slide 1, options/hint on 2, answer on last slide. Rationale: swipes add dwell time and the unswiped "second chance" re-show; saves are the carousel's strongest metric (Socialinsider, Metricool). Last slide CTA: "send to someone who'd get it wrong".
- Reels: question on screen immediately, visible timer or countdown, reveal at the end. Rationale: watch time to the reveal and replays are the Reel signal; sends via "challenge a friend".
- Hook risk for hands-free voice app: do not lead with app promo. Content must stand alone; app mention only in bio/last frame while "coming soon". [A]

## 4. When to change the mix (milestones, own-data driven)

| Milestone | Change |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1-4 (0-199 followers) | 5 Reels + 2 carousels, no Stories effort. Log median reach and sends per reach per format weekly |
| 200 followers (professional account) | Turn on Trial Reels for new hook/format experiments (Reels only); keep proven formats as normal posts |
| Own data after 4 weeks: carousel median reach >= Reel median | Move to 3 Reels + 4 carousels (conflicting public data, see 3.2) |
| One Reel/format clearly beats others on sends per reach | Repeat that format weekly; kill the weakest question type |
| ~1,000 followers | Add 3 Stories/week (quiz sticker, poll, "reply with your answer") to retain and convert; consider carousel share up toward 40-50% (Socialinsider: carousels lead saves/views as accounts grow) |
| ~5-10K followers | Carousels likely overtake Reels on views at larger sizes in Socialinsider data; rebalance to about 50/50, keep 1-2 Trial Reels per week for discovery |
| Output quality drops | Cut to 3-4 posts/week rather than skip weeks |
