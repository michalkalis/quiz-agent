  1. Point Nemo in the South Pacific is the spot in the ocean farthest from any land, about 2,700 kilometres in every direction. When a ship passes through, the nearest other humans are often not on land or sea at all. Where are they?

    Show answer

    On the International Space Station

    The station orbits only about 400 kilometres up, so when it passes overhead its crew are far closer to Point Nemo than anyone on shore.

    Source: snopes.com

  2. The Canary Islands weren't named after the little yellow bird. The bird was named after the islands. So which animal did the islands' name originally come from?

    Show answer

    Dogs

    The Romans called them 'Canariae Insulae', the Islands of the Dogs, from the Latin 'canis', after the big dogs found there. That's why dogs, not birds, appear on the islands' coat of arms.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  3. Rio de Janeiro means 'River of January', yet the city has no big river. What did the Portuguese sailors who named it actually mistake for a river?

    Show answer

    A bay

    In January 1502 the sailors took the narrow entrance of Guanabara Bay for the mouth of a large river. The mistaken name has stuck for over five centuries.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  4. Early explorers were sure a South American land was full of one precious metal, so they named it after that metal's Latin name, and the country still carries it today. Which metal is hidden inside the name 'Argentina'?

    Show answer

    Silver

    Argentina comes from the Latin 'argentum', the same word behind silver's chemical symbol Ag. The great silver riches the explorers dreamed of never really turned up there.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  5. Which independent kingdom shares its entire land border with South Africa and no other country?

    Show answer

    Lesotho

    Lesotho is one of only three countries in the world completely enclosed by a single neighbour, alongside Vatican City and San Marino inside Italy.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  6. France's longest land border isn't with any European country at all. Which country is it shared with?

    Show answer

    Brazil

    French Guiana in South America is a full part of France, and its border with Brazil runs about 730 kilometres, longer than France's border with Spain. So in a sense, Brazil is France's biggest neighbour.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  7. Only one US state has a genuine royal palace, built for a real king and queen. Which state is it?

    Show answer

    Hawaii

    Hawaii was an independent kingdom until 1893, and ʻIolani Palace in Honolulu was the home of its last monarchs. It even had electric lights before the White House did.

    Source: nps.gov

  8. Venice's grand palaces and churches stand on millions of what, driven deep into the mud of the lagoon?

    Show answer

    Wooden piles

    Venice rests on a forest of wooden stakes, and many of them have stood for centuries. Sealed underwater in oxygen-poor mud, the wood is protected from the microbes that would otherwise rot it.

    Source: britannica.com

  9. Which river passes through or along the borders of ten different countries, more than any other river in the world?

    Show answer

    Danube

    From Germany's Black Forest to the Black Sea, the Danube touches ten countries and flows through four capitals: Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest and Belgrade.

    Source: britannica.com

  10. Which country has the longest coastline of any nation on Earth?

    Show answer

    Canada

    Canada's coastline runs to roughly 200,000 kilometres, mostly because its Arctic archipelago is made up of thousands of islands with wildly indented shores.

    Source: worldatlas.com

  11. The world's largest island is a self-governing territory of which European kingdom?

    Show answer

    Denmark

    Greenland is more than fifty times the size of Denmark itself, yet it remains part of the Danish realm with its own home-rule government.

    Source: commonslibrary.parliament.uk

  12. Measured from its base on the sea floor to its summit, which Hawaiian volcano is taller than Mount Everest?

    Show answer

    Mauna Kea

    Only about 4,200 metres of it stand above the sea, but the volcano rises more than 10,000 metres from the ocean floor, over a kilometre more than Everest.

    Source: mossandfog.com

  13. Which single lake, in Siberia, holds roughly one fifth of all the unfrozen fresh water on the Earth's surface?

    Show answer

    Lake Baikal

    At over 1,600 metres, it is the deepest lake on Earth, so it stores more fresh water than all five North American Great Lakes combined.

    Source: whc.unesco.org

  14. Which continent contains more sovereign countries than any other?

    Show answer

    Africa

    Africa has 54 internationally recognised countries, comfortably ahead of Europe and Asia.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  15. One Nordic capital's name means 'smoky bay', because early settlers mistook the steam from its hot springs for smoke. Which city is it?

    Show answer

    Reykjavík

    In Icelandic, 'reykur' means smoke and 'vík' means bay. Today those same hot springs heat most of the city's homes.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  16. Legend says the Aztecs built their capital where they saw an eagle perched on a cactus, eating a snake. That scene is now on a national flag. Which capital city stands on that spot today?

    Show answer

    Mexico City

    Mexico City was built on top of the Aztec capital Tenochtitlan, which stood on an island in a lake. The eagle, cactus and snake from the founding legend sit in the middle of the Mexican flag.

    Source: about.jstor.org

  17. Mozambique's flag shows a farmer's hoe and an open book, plus a third object you won't find on any other national flag. What is it?

    Show answer

    An AK-47 rifle

    The Kalashnikov stands for defence and the armed struggle for independence, with the hoe for agriculture and the book for education. Proposals to remove the rifle have come up several times, but it is still on the flag.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  18. Only one US state shares a land border with exactly one other state. Which state is it?

    Show answer

    Maine

    Maine, in the far north-east corner, is boxed in by Canada and the Atlantic, so New Hampshire is its only US neighbour. Alaska and Hawaii don't count, because they border no states at all.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  19. Wine corks are cut from the bark of an oak that grows in warm, dry Mediterranean land, and the bark is peeled off every nine years without cutting the tree down. Which country produces about half of the world's cork?

    Show answer

    Portugal

    Portugal's cork oak forests supply around half of all the world's cork. The tree is so valued there that it is protected by law and cutting one down without permission is illegal.

    Source: amorimcork.com

  20. Mount Everest's summit sits exactly on the border between Nepal and which other country?

    Show answer

    China

    The northern side of Everest lies in Tibet, which is part of China, and climbers can reach the top from either the Nepalese or the Chinese side.

    Source: britannica.com
