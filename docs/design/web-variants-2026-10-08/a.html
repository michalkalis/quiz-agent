How it works

Questions read aloud, answers checked by meaning.

Hands free

Trubbo reads the question and listens for your answer. Nobody has to hold the phone or read cards out.

Say it your way

AI checks whether your answer is right. You don't need the exact wording the question was written with.

Every question is checked

Questions are prepared in advance. Each one has a source and is fact-checked before it gets into the game.

Your categories

Pick the categories you like. Content stays suitable for the age of the players, so kids can join.

English, Slovak, Czech

Play in any of the three. Questions are read in the language you choose.

Packs and custom packs

Buy a question pack on a theme, or order a custom pack on a topic you pick.
