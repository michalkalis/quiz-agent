  1. A new Resident Evil film reached cinemas in 2026, but in Japan, where the games are made, the series has never used that name. What is it called there?

    Show answer

    Biohazard

    The games were renamed for the West because of trademark problems with the name Biohazard. Japan has kept the original title ever since.

    Source: capcom.fandom.com

  2. In September 2026 the Indian gangster saga Mirzapur jumped to cinemas as a feature film. On which streaming service did it begin as a series in 2018?

    Show answer

    Amazon Prime Video

    Mirzapur became one of Prime Video's most-watched Indian originals. Its big-screen move reversed the usual journey from film to streaming.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  3. Marvel's Wolverine became a big PlayStation 5 release in September 2026. What virtually unbreakable fictional metal coats Wolverine's skeleton and claws?

    Show answer

    Adamantium

    The metal was bonded to his bones by the secret Weapon X programme. Only his healing factor let him survive the procedure.

    Source: playstation.com

  4. A new Resident Evil film was the biggest movie in British and Irish cinemas in September 2026. In the games, which sinister pharmaceutical company created the virus that turns people into zombies?

    Show answer

    Umbrella Corporation

    Umbrella's T-virus leaked in Raccoon City and started the whole outbreak. Its red-and-white logo is one of gaming's most recognisable corporate symbols.

    Source: screendaily.com

  5. After Dahmer, the Menendez brothers and Ed Gein, Netflix's true-crime anthology Monster turned to a Massachusetts woman tried in 1893 for killing her father and stepmother with an axe. Who is she?

    Show answer

    Lizzie Borden

    Lizzie Borden was acquitted, but a skipping rhyme about giving her mother 'forty whacks' made her infamous for good. Ella Beatty plays her in the Netflix season.

    Source: thewrap.com

  6. In 2026's The Social Reckoning, Succession star Jeremy Strong plays Mark Zuckerberg. Which actor first played the Facebook founder back in 2010, and later played Superman's enemy Lex Luthor?

    Show answer

    Jesse Eisenberg

    Jesse Eisenberg played Zuckerberg in The Social Network and then Lex Luthor in Batman v Superman. For the follow-up, the role passed to Jeremy Strong.

    Source: variety.com

  7. In the 2026 film Coyote vs. Acme, Wile E. Coyote finally stops blaming himself for decades of exploding gadgets and failed traps. What does he do to the company that sold them to him?

    Show answer

    Sues it

    He hires a human lawyer and takes Acme to court for product liability. The finished film was shelved by its own studio, then rescued and released by another distributor.

    Source: nerdtropolis.com

  8. At the 2026 Emmys both supporting acting awards for a limited series went to the same HBO show, DTF St. Louis. David Harbour took one. The other went to an actress who played Velma in the live-action Scooby-Doo films and Lindsay Weir in Freaks and Geeks. Who is she?

    Show answer

    Linda Cardellini

    The two-hander about a middle-aged romance also won Outstanding Limited Series, giving HBO a clean sweep of the show's three biggest categories that night.

    Source: nbcboston.com

  9. Sally Field won the 2026 Emmy for lead actress in a limited series for Remarkably Bright Creatures, adapted from Shelby Van Pelt's bestselling novel. Her character, a widow cleaning an aquarium at night, forms a bond with what kind of eight-limbed sea creature?

    Show answer

    An octopus

    The octopus, Marcellus, even narrates parts of the novel and helps the widow unravel the mystery of her missing son.

    Source: wunc.org

  10. The 2026 Primetime Emmys had their first female host in fifteen years: an actress who has played detective Olivia Benson in Law and Order: Special Victims Unit since 1999. Who is she?

    Show answer

    Mariska Hargitay

    Hargitay was the first woman to host the Emmys since Jane Lynch in 2011, and she did it while still starring in the longest-running live-action drama on American television.

    Source: hollywoodlife.com

  11. At Disney's D23 showcase in August 2026, Marvel revealed the cast of its new X-Men film. Which actor, previously the masked villain Kylo Ren in the Star Wars sequels, was announced as the X-Men villain Mister Sinister?

    Show answer

    Adam Driver

    Driver joins a young mutant line-up that includes Sadie Sink as Jean Grey and Kit Connor as Cyclops, so he swaps one franchise's dark side for another's.

    Source: local10.com

  12. The Ig Nobel Prizes, the spoof awards for research that first makes you laugh and then makes you think, were held outside the United States for the first time in 2026. In which Swiss city?

    Show answer

    Zurich

    The 2026 ceremony moved to Zurich after more than three decades in the US. The plan is to keep travelling, with Antwerp next.

    Source: improbable.com
