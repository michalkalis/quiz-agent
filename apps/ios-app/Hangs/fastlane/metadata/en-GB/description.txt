Trubbo reads each question aloud, you answer in your own words and hear right away whether you got it. No tapping or reading, so anyone nearby can play, for example friends at home, the kids or everyone in the car.

• Questions from everyday life, science, history, sport, film, food and more
• Answer your own way, Trubbo understands what you mean
• A short explanation and a source link after every answer
• Every question is checked against sources before you play it
• Your own pack on any topic, for example space for kids or 90s music. You can start playing almost right away
• Play in English, Slovak or Czech. You can also type your answers

30 free questions every month. Trubbo Plus gives you 1,000 questions a month, and a one-time pack of 100 questions never expires. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Playing in the car? Drive safe. Questions are created with the help of AI and checked against sources, but a mistake can still slip through now and then. You can export or delete your data anytime in Settings.
