  1. Before you start

    Pick the categories and the language.

    Choose from categories like history, science, sports or food. Play in English, Slovak or Czech. Content stays suitable for the age of the players, so kids can play along.

  2. Step 1

    Trubbo reads the question aloud.

    Everyone hears it at the same time, including whoever is driving or cooking. Nobody needs to look at the phone.

  3. Step 2

    You answer out loud.

    Just say it. If talking isn't an option, tap the answer instead.

  4. Step 3

    AI checks what you meant.

    “I think it's Canberra” counts. The answer is judged by meaning, not by exact match. Every question has a source and was fact-checked before it got into the game.

    Every question has a source
  5. Step 4

    Then the next question.

    At the end of the round you see how you did and can go back over the answers. Road trip, party or an evening at the cabin, it works wherever people have time together.
