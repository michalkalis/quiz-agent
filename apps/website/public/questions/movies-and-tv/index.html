  1. In Disney's The Lion King, the wise baboon mentor Rafiki has a name that is an ordinary Swahili word. What does it mean?

    Show answer

    Friend

    'Rafiki' simply means friend in Swahili. The same language gave the film 'Hakuna Matata', which roughly means 'no worries'.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  2. Rowan Atkinson's almost wordless Mr. Bean is loved all over the world. Which classic small British car does he drive, once even steering it from an armchair strapped to its roof?

    Show answer

    Mini

    Bean's lime-green Mini with its black bonnet became almost a co-star. It is padlocked shut and battles a little blue three-wheeler that always ends up on its side.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  3. Which 1990s sitcom made the word 'regifting' famous, in an episode where a dentist passes on a label maker he was given?

    Show answer

    Seinfeld

    Elaine catches the dentist Tim Whatley handing her own present on to Jerry and labels him a 'regifter'. The word caught on and is now in major dictionaries.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  4. In Breaking Bad, chemistry teacher Walter White takes a criminal alias borrowed from a famous German physicist. That physicist is best known for a principle named after what?

    Show answer

    Uncertainty

    The alias is Heisenberg, after Werner Heisenberg's uncertainty principle. It suits a man whose true identity the police can never be sure of.

    Source: looper.com

  5. Which space-fleet TV series let its characters swear constantly on air by inventing the curse word 'frak'?

    Show answer

    Battlestar Galactica

    Because 'frak' wasn't a real word, censors couldn't touch it, so the crew could say it in nearly every episode. It became so popular that fans still use it as a real-world polite curse.

    Source: syfy.com

  6. Alan Rickman had never appeared in a feature film until, at 41, he played a suave villain holding a Los Angeles skyscraper hostage. Which 1988 action movie was it?

    Show answer

    Die Hard

    Rickman was a Royal Shakespeare Company stage actor with no film credits when he was cast as Hans Gruber, and the role launched his Hollywood career overnight.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  7. The Lion King's carefree song 'Hakuna Matata' takes its title from a real phrase meaning 'no worries' in which African language?

    Show answer

    Swahili

    Swahili is spoken across East Africa, including Kenya and Tanzania, whose savannas inspired the film's setting.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  8. Which actor was working as a carpenter before being cast as Han Solo in the original Star Wars?

    Show answer

    Harrison Ford

    Ford said he was quite surprised to be offered the part. His carpentry skills reportedly helped him land studio work where he was noticed.

    Source: factinate.com

  9. The first draft script for which blockbuster vampire saga featured its heroine as a star athlete being chased by the FBI on jet skis?

    Show answer

    Twilight

    Director Catherine Hardwicke binned that action-movie draft, which she said had nothing to do with the book, and steered the film back to the moody romance that launched a billion-dollar franchise.

    Source: eonline.com

  10. Which film took home more Academy Awards: James Cameron's Titanic or the 1959 epic Ben-Hur?

    Show answer

    Neither, they tied

    Both epics won exactly eleven Oscars, so Titanic could only tie the record Ben-Hur had held for almost forty years, even while topping the US box office for fifteen straight weeks and earning over two point one billion dollars.

    Source: britannica.com

  11. Which 1995 film was the first full-length feature made entirely with computer animation?

    Show answer

    Toy Story

    Pixar's debut feature took roughly four years to make and turned a cowboy doll and a space ranger into the start of a new era in animation.

    Source: oscars.org

  12. In the Pixar film Finding Nemo, what kind of fish are Nemo and his father Marlin?

    Show answer

    Clownfish

    Clownfish live among the stinging tentacles of sea anemones, which is exactly where the film's family makes its home.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  13. The mechanical shark in Steven Spielberg's Jaws was given a friendly first-name nickname on set, borrowed from Spielberg's lawyer. What was the shark called?

    Show answer

    Bruce

    Bruce broke down so often in the salt water that Spielberg was forced to keep the shark off screen, which made the film far scarier.

    Source: newsweek.com

  14. Two years before Marlon Brando famously refused his Best Actor Oscar for The Godfather, another actor had already turned his down, won for playing a Second World War general. Who was he?

    Show answer

    George C. Scott

    Scott called the Oscars a 'two-hour meat parade' and declined the award for Patton in 1971, becoming the first actor ever to refuse one.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org

  15. For decades, film and TV makers often avoided showing characters sing 'Happy Birthday to You', or cut it off after a line. What was the reason?

    Show answer

    It was under copyright

    A music publisher claimed to own the song and charged fees for using it, earning around 2 million dollars a year. In 2015 a US judge ruled that the claim was invalid, and the song has been free to sing ever since.

    Source: en.wikipedia.org
