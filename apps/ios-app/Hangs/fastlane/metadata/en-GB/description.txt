Trubbo is a trivia quiz you play with your voice. It reads each question aloud, you answer out loud in your own words, and you hear right away whether you got it. Questions are read aloud, so everyone around hears them and anyone can answer, for example at home with friends, with the kids or on a road trip.

HOW IT WORKS
• A natural voice reads every question
• Answer in your own words. Trubbo understands what you mean, not only the exact wording
• Multiple choice questions: say the answer or its number
• Control the quiz by voice: start, ok, next, repeat, skip
• After every answer you get a short explanation and a link to the source

PICK YOUR QUIZ
• Categories from science and history to sports, film and food
• Easy, medium or hard questions
• Play in English, Slovak or Czech
• Prefer typing? Every answer can be entered on screen too

YOUR OWN PACK
Describe any topic, for example space for kids or 90s music, and Trubbo writes a pack of 30 questions for you. The first questions are ready to play within minutes.

FREE OR TRUBBO PLUS
30 questions free every month. The Trubbo Plus subscription gives you 1,000 questions a month. A one-time pack of 100 questions never expires. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

GOOD TO KNOW
Playing in the car? Drive safe.
Questions and answer evaluation are created with the help of AI and can contain mistakes. You can export or delete your data anytime in Settings.
