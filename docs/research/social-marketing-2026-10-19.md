# Social media marketing: tools, channels, language (2026-10-19)

**Ask (founder, 2026-10-19):** research and plan marketing to grow visibility and pack/subscription purchases. Start Instagram with mostly AI-generated content (motion reels, stories), add other channels, maybe a personal "player" account. Everything free if possible.

Builds on the unmerged launch playbook (PR #148 — organic App Store launch marketing: ASO first, Apple featuring nominations, Product Hunt weak, Reddit rules, Apple Ads test, nano-influencer barter). Not repeated here. Plan: [issue-210](../issues/issue-210-social-marketing.md).

Evidence labels: **official** (platform docs) · **data** (study with numbers) · **secondary** (blog/vendor summary) · **UNVERIFIED** (single weak source or not found).

## TL;DR

- **Content is free if rendered in code, not generated by video AI.** Remotion (free for companies of ≤3 people, official Claude Code skills) turns a question row from our DB into a 9:16 reel: question, voice, countdown, answer, explanation. Every free AI-video tier is non-commercial, watermarked or tiny.
- **Instagram can be fully automated for free and without a third party** via the official Instagram API (own account, no app review). Stories with poll/link stickers cannot be posted by any API — those stay a 30-second manual tap. TikTok's API keeps self-built posters private-only, so TikTok goes via Buffer free or by hand.
- **Claude Code ecosystem:** `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (MIT, 55k★: social, video, aso, launch, attribution, paywalls) and Anthropic's official `marketing` plugin for brand voice/planning. Neither posts anything; posting is our own small script.
- **Formats with the best odds:** timer reel ("guess before the monkey reveals it"), question carousels (on Instagram carousels beat reels on saves/sends), real "kids vs parents in the car" clips, real crowd numbers from the daily challenge (#205 — daily challenge vs the crowd), "comment a topic → we build a custom pack".
- **Realistic conversion:** one indie data point ≈ 1 install per 100–150 views. Plan for volume and many hooks, not one viral hit.
- **Driving safety is a brand risk, not just a legal one.** Driver never touches or looks at the phone on camera; passenger films or the car is parked. YouTube and TikTok remove dangerous-driving content.
- **No evidence found that mixed-language accounts are penalised** (corrects PR #148's claim). Language choice is a market-size question — see the market sizing section.

## 3. Formats

Evidence is thin: no named trivia account with verified numbers was found; formats below are test designs.

- **Data:** TikTok views per post fell 31 % year on year as volume surged ([Metricool, 2.31M posts](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)). Instagram carousels get 4.7× the views and 9× the saves of single images ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)). Instagram ranks on watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** — trivia is built for "send this to the person who'd get it wrong".
- **Data:** Trivia Royale got 2.5M downloads in 3 weeks from unscripted creators playing the game on TikTok ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/10/how-thor-fridrikssons-trivia-royale-earned-2-5m-downloads-in-3-weeks/)). Dumbphone: 2.4M views → 20k downloads in 78 days, ≈ 0.8 % ([Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/20k-downloads-and-9k-mrr-in-78-days-how-this-indie-app-turned-minimalism-into-millions-of-views)).
- **Policy:** Instagram's April 2026 originality change stops recommending accounts that mostly share non-original content ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)); Mosseri favours raw, human content over AI polish ([BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2026/01/01/adam-mosseri-suggests-highlighting-real-media-rather-than-ai-content-on-social-media/)). Our template reels are original, but identical-looking output risks reading as mass-produced → vary hooks, add real clips.
- **AI labels:** cartoon/motion graphics are not "realistic synthetic media" on Meta, TikTok or YouTube. A realistic AI voice may count as realistic audio on TikTok — disclose when in doubt. EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency applies from 2026-08-02 (scope UNVERIFIED).

| # | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Timer reel: voice asks, 5 s countdown, monkey reveals, one-line "why" | Native to the product, fully automatable |
| 2 | Question carousel ("9 questions, answers on the last slide"; API cap 10 items) | Carousels win saves/sends on IG; fully automatable |
| 3 | Kids vs parents / couple in the car (real, passenger films) | Highest shareability; human content the algorithm prefers |
| 4 | "Only 23 % of Trubbo players knew this" — real numbers from #205 | Honest version of the "1 % get this" bait |
| 5 | Comment a topic → custom pack built and played next video | Showcases the €8.99 custom pack (hypothesis) |
| 6 | Stories: daily question with quiz/poll sticker | Keeps followers active; manual tap |

## 4. Founder-led content and driving safety

- No data compares personal vs brand accounts for installs. Indie examples (Edward Sturm, Cal AI) grew via founder-led short video ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/creators/meet-the-indie-hackers-killing-it-on-tiktok-3znZ4MnXFrhlr5CCVuio)). Cal AI's founder warns against showing the pipeline (clone risk) — share story and results, not prompts.
- **SK law:** holding or operating the phone while driving is the offence, whatever the activity ([NSS SR, 2026-01-22](https://www.nssud.sk/en/using-a-telephone-while-driving-also-includes-holding-the-telephone-and-using-any-of-its-functions/)). **CZ:** similar, 1 500–2 500 CZK + points (UNVERIFIED whether the 2023 reform raising it is in force). Passenger filming is not covered by either ban (UNVERIFIED, not legal advice).
- **Platforms:** [YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801964) removes "extremely dangerous driving"; [TikTok](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-08-24/no-10-urges-social-media-firms-to-remove-videos-glorifying-dangerous-driving) treats dangerous driving as a violation.

## 6. Converting viewers to buyers

- Social traffic should land on a **custom product page** matching the video (PR #148: road trip / party / family).
- Subscription conversion per paywall view peaks in **January** (3.34 %) and dips before Black Friday ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/state-of-holidays-for-subscription-apps/)) — New Year is the stronger promo moment than Black Friday.
- No published lift data for seasonal or limited-time packs (gap) → run them as experiments: one topical pack every 3–4 weeks with its own reel.
- In-app share card after a daily challenge closes the loop (Wordle precedent).

## 7. Market size: SK/CZ vs English

Back-of-envelope estimate; installs are assumptions, the rest sourced. Any row can swing 2–3×.

- **iPhone users** (population × 80 % smartphone × [StatCounter iOS share](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/czech-republic)): SK ~1.4M, CZ ~2.6M, US ~147M, UK ~29M, CA+AU+IE ~37M. English markets ≈ 50× SK+CZ.
- **Funnel** ([RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)): download → paid by day 35 median 2.0 % (freemium 2.1 %, gaming 1.0 %, North America 2.6–2.8 %); year-1 value per payer ~$23–26.
- **Paid ads don't pay in English markets:** Apple Ads cost per install US $2.51, UK $2.02, IE $1.02 ([Adapty 2026](https://adapty.io/blog/apple-ads-benchmarks-2026/)) → $120–250 per payer against ~$25 year-1 revenue. CZ/SK figures not published (cheap per PR #148, UNVERIFIED).
- **Competition:** US trivia is crowded (Elevate, Trivia Crack, Kahoot — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-trivia-games-revenue-us-604118ed241bc16eb8b8453a)); CZ has a few small indie quiz apps, no voice/driving one found.
- **SK and CZ behave as one market:** Slovak creators' audiences are ~76 % SK + ~17 % CZ ([Modash](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/tiktok/slovakia)).

| Year 1, organic only | Payers low / mid / high | Revenue low / mid / high |
|---|---|---|
| SK + CZ | 14 / 72 / 375 | €0.2K / €1.1K / €6.8K |
| English (US, UK, CA, AU, IE) | 40 / 300 / 1 800 | €0.6K / €5.4K / €39.6K |

**Conclusion (founder decision 2026-10-19):** English is the main account (≈5× mid, ≈6× upside; differentiator vs crowded text trivia = hands-free driving quiz). SK/CZ runs as a secondary account from the same pipeline at near-zero extra cost — an uncontested store to validate conversion. No paid ads in English markets; an optional ~€50–100 Apple Ads test CZ/SK vs IE/CA only with founder approval.
