Classic landing. Hero with a fanned deck of category cards; the top card cycles real questions and a glass "Listening" pill shows the spoken answer. Then a colored tile grid of facts, places, pricing and an open beta panel.

A single scrolling story. A sticky game screen walks through a round as you scroll: pick categories, question read aloud, you answer, AI checks the meaning, next question. Pricing and packs come after in three short boxes.

Compact. The first screen is one glass sheet over a table of drifting question cards, holding the pitch, the beta button, price and languages. Below it, short reference blocks (how it plays, the questions, pricing) and an FAQ.
