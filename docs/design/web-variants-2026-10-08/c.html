Questions

Do I have to talk?

No. Voice is the main way to play, but you can also answer by tapping.

What if I say the answer a bit differently?

That's fine. AI checks whether your answer means the right thing instead of matching exact words.

Where do the questions come from?

They are prepared in advance. Every question has a source and is fact-checked before it is added.
