# The seven quiz categories (pick exactly one id per question)

Players pick one of these in the app to hear questions about that subject. File each question where a player who picked that category would expect it.

- `science-nature` (Science & Nature): physics, chemistry, biology, the human body and medicine, animals and plants, space, weather, maths, and technology (inventions, gadgets, the internet, how everyday things work), including the science of food (why milk boils over, why chili burns, what plant vanilla comes from).
- `history` (History): past events, eras, rulers, wars, famous people of the past, and where inventions, objects, customs and words came from.
- `geography-world` (Geography): countries, cities, landmarks, rivers, mountains, flags, languages, peoples, and how life, customs and holidays are kept around the world today.
- `movies-music` (Movies & Music): films, television series, actors, directors, music, musicians, songs, soundtracks.
- `sports` (Sports): every sport, athletes, clubs, competitions, records, rules.
- `food-everyday` (Food & Drink): food and drink ONLY: dishes, ingredients, cooking and recipes, drinks, restaurants and bars, food brands, food customs and food history. Not cars, shopping, household, clothing or general daily life, and not the chemistry or biology behind food (that is `science-nature`).
- `entertainment` (Pop Culture): celebrities, video games and board/card games, internet culture and social media, books and comics, toys, fashion, brands and advertising, recent entertainment news.

## Routing rules for the confusable subjects (choose the closest subject; every question fits one of the seven)

- Word origins and names: file by where the story is: a place or people → `geography-world`; a dish or drink → `food-everyday`; a film/song → `movies-music`; an animal, plant, body part or scientific thing → `science-nature`; otherwise (the word's past, a historical person or event) → `history`.
- Customs and holidays: how they are kept in a country or around the world today → `geography-world`; what a custom originally meant or was for, how/when it began, old folk beliefs, the historical person behind it → `history`; festive dishes and drinks → `food-everyday`.
- Brands and companies: food and drink brands → `food-everyday`; technology/gadget/internet companies → `science-nature`; other brands, logos, mascots, toys, fashion → `entertainment`; a company's origin story → `history`.
- Everyday life (household, cars, money, shopping, transport, rules of daily life): how a thing works → `science-nature`; where/when it came from → `history`; how a particular country does it (laws, services, habits) → `geography-world`.
- Language trivia that is about a country's language as such (grammar, alphabet, how people speak) → `geography-world`.
- Books, writers, comics → `entertainment` (pop culture), unless the question is really about a historical event or person → `history`. Classical art, painters and old architecture → `history`; a landmark as a place to visit → `geography-world`.
- A question about a dish, drink, ingredient, cooking or eating is `food-everyday` even when it mentions a country or holiday, unless it asks for the science behind it (`science-nature`); a question that only mentions food in passing goes to its real subject.
