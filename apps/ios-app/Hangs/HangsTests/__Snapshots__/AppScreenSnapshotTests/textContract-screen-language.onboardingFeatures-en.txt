VOICE CONTROL
Say "start"
Say "start" after a question, or tap Answer, to begin answering.
Five simple words
start · ok · next · repeat · skip. That's all you need.
English by default
Commands are spoken in English by default. Set your quiz language to Slovak or Czech in Settings to speak them in that language.
Buttons always work
Every command also has an on-screen button. You don't have to use your voice.
