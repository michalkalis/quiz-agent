Trubbo is a trivia quiz you play with your voice. It reads each question aloud, you answer out loud in your own words, and you hear right away whether you got it. No tapping and no reading, so you can play with friends, with the kids, while cooking or on a road trip.

HOW IT WORKS
• A natural voice reads every question
• Answer in your own words. Trubbo understands what you mean, not only the exact wording
• Multiple choice questions: say the answer or its number
• Control the quiz by voice: start, ok, next, repeat, skip
• After every answer you get a short explanation and a link to the source

PICK YOUR QUIZ
• Categories from science and history to sports, film and food
• Easy, medium or hard questions
• Play in English, Slovak or Czech
• Prefer typing? Every answer can be entered on screen too

YOUR OWN PACK
Describe any topic, for example space for kids or 90s music, and Trubbo writes a pack of 30 questions for you. Custom packs are in English for now. The first questions are ready to play within minutes.

FREE OR UNLIMITED
30 questions free every month. The Unlimited subscription removes the monthly limit. A one-time pack of 100 questions never expires. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

SAFETY
If you play in the car, the driver should use voice only. Never operate your phone while driving and always follow traffic laws.

GOOD TO KNOW
Questions and answer evaluation are created with the help of AI and can contain mistakes. You can export or delete your data anytime in Settings.
