Privacy Policy · Trubbo

Effective: October 10, 2026

Who we are

Trubbo (a voice-first trivia quiz app) is operated by missinghue s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 07288093, with its registered office at Kotěrova 1588/2a, Dejvice, 160 00 Prague 6, Czech Republic ("we"). We are the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@missinghue.com.

What we process

  • Account & identifiers: an anonymous device identifier and sign-in tokens created on first launch. If you optionally sign in with Apple, we also process the stable identifier Apple assigns to you; we do not request your name or e-mail from Apple.
  • Voice: recordings of your answers and voice commands are processed solely to convert speech to text. Answer recordings are not stored permanently (exception: the test version with your consent, see below); we store the text of your answer as part of your quiz history. If you submit voice feedback, we keep that recording until the feedback is handled.
  • Quiz activity: answers, scores, monthly usage counts (for the free-tier limit), question ratings.
  • Purchases: subscription and pack status. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports (Sentry) so we can fix problems.
  • Usage statistics: in-app events (for example a started quiz or a viewed subscription offer), stored in our own database without advertising identifiers, so we can improve the app.
  • Custom packs and feedback: the topic you enter when ordering a pack, and the feedback you send us (text, screenshot and, if you choose, a voice message).

We show no ads and do not track you across other companies' apps.

Answer recordings in the test version

This applies only to the test version of the app (TestFlight). The App Store version never asks and never sends recordings.

  • Only with consent: before your first quiz we ask you once whether you want to send us recordings of your answers. Nothing is sent unless you say “Yes”.
  • Purpose: improving speech recognition and answer grading. We use the recordings for automated tests, for nothing else, and never sell them.
  • What is sent: the audio of your spoken answer and technical details about it (language, question, recognized text, grading result, microphone type), linked to your anonymous device identifier or your account.
  • Storage: a private Cloudflare R2 storage bucket that only the developer can access.
  • Retention: at most 12 months from recording, then deleted. Deleting your data or account deletes them immediately.
  • Withdrawing consent: turn off the “Send answer recordings” switch in Settings at any time. Recordings already sent are deleted via “Delete my data” or by email.

Artificial intelligence

Trubbo is an AI-powered app: quiz questions are AI-generated and your spoken answers are evaluated by AI. Content may therefore contain inaccuracies. You are interacting with an automated system, not a human.

Legal bases and purposes

  • Contract: providing the quiz, evaluating answers, managing subscriptions and limits.
  • Legitimate interest: diagnostics, usage statistics, security, abuse prevention.
  • Consent: microphone access (granted via the iOS system dialog, revocable any time in Settings) and sending answer recordings in the test version.

Recipients (processors)

We rely on these providers: Apple (distribution, payments, sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription management), ElevenLabs (speech-to-text), OpenAI and Anthropic/OpenRouter (processing answer text, generating and evaluating quiz content, speech synthesis), Sentry (error diagnostics), Cloudflare (storage of answer recordings in the test version), Fly.io (hosting; EU servers in Paris). Data is shared only to the extent necessary for each purpose.

Retention and deletion

Account data and quiz history are kept while you use the app. You can delete your data at any time directly in the app (Settings → Delete my data) or by e-mail. After deletion, your personal data is removed without undue delay. Purchase records are kept for as long as accounting rules and refund handling require. Your answers and question ratings are kept only in anonymised form for statistics, and pack orders are kept without the link to your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Exercise them via hello@missinghue.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz) or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

Children

The app is not directed at children without adult supervision; the App Store age rating applies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version will always be available at this address with its effective date.
