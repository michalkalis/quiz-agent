# Issue #210 — Marketing: sociálne siete s AI obsahom (Instagram, YouTube Shorts; TikTok later)

**Triage:** enhancement · ready-for-agent

**Created:** 2026-10-19 · **Founder:** Michal · **Source:** [research](../research/social-marketing-2026-10-19.md) (tools, channels, market size) + launch playbook PR #148 (organic App Store launch marketing: ASO, Apple featuring, Reddit, nano-influencers)

## Goal

More visibility and more pack / Trubbo Plus purchases through short-form social content that is mostly generated by an agent from our question database, at zero tool cost. Success after 4 weeks of posting: a weekly batch is produced and approved on one page, Instagram publishes itself, every channel has its own App Store campaign link, and we have per-language numbers (views per post, installs per channel) to decide where to put effort next.

## Founder decisions (2026-10-19)

1. **Language: English main + SK/CZ secondary.** Brand account @trubbo in English (≈5× the SK+CZ revenue in the mid estimate, ≈6× upside). One shared SK/CZ account from the same pipeline (native sk and cs questions, one language per video). Compare after 4 weeks.
2. **People:** brand account + a **new** personal account (not the founder's existing one), in **English**: the solo-dev story and the founder playing the quiz. The monkey is the recurring character on the brand account.
3. **Content:** mostly AI/code-generated; **occasionally real clips** (~1 per week) of the **founder playing the app himself and answering out loud** (screen recording + his voice, optionally his face). **No videos from the car** (founder, 2026-10-19, follow-up). Driving appears only in AI/motion content.
4. **Approval:** the agent prepares a **weekly batch** (5–7 posts per account with captions); the founder approves it on one page, then it schedules itself.
5. Free tools only. No paid ads in English markets (a payer would cost $120–250 vs ~$25 year-1 revenue).

## Founder decisions (2026-10-23)

6. **TikTok deferred, not dropped.** TikTok asks for a phone number and the founder's only number is on his personal account; it comes back (EN + SK/CZ) when he has a second number. Channels now: Instagram EN, Instagram SK/CZ, YouTube Shorts.

YouTube: own Data API project; Google locks API uploads to private until the project passes its free compliance audit, so Shorts go through Buffer free (already set up, an audited client) only until the audit passes, then Buffer is dropped.

## Founder decisions (2026-10-22) — direction after the [growth research](../research/growth-strategy-2026-10-21.md)

After App Store launch: shareable result card, product page (screenshots, custom product pages, ratings prompt). After 2–4 weeks of data: offer shape, maybe a small Apple Ads CZ/SK test.
2. **Content production: no paid external services or models.** Remotion (free licence), existing ElevenLabs question clips only (skip questions without audio), captions by the agent, local tools only.
3. **No hard paywall, ever** (founder hates them and uninstalls such apps). Stay as free as possible; a trial is acceptable to discuss later.
4. **Start posting now**, before the App Store launch: bios say "coming soon" + link to trubbo.app (public beta CTA already there).
5. **Apple featuring nomination: later**, for the version that is actually launched after real testing (1.1 has only been tested by the founder). 1.1 stays on automatic release after approval (founder, 2026-10-22).

## Hard rules

- **Driving safety:** no real footage from a moving car. Any driving visual (AI/motion) never shows a driver touching or looking at a phone — one such clip damages the whole brand and gets removed on YouTube/TikTok.
- **Only approved questions** (App Store rule: state `approved`), each with its source; the explanation line comes from the question's own explanation, not invented.
- **Real numbers only** in "X % of players knew this" posts (from #205 — daily challenge vs the crowd), never estimated.
- **Copy:** no cheesy filler, no dashes as punctuation in public text, sk/cs written natively (copy-style rules apply to captions too).
- **Credentials:** never give any tool an Instagram password or cookies; official API tokens only, in `.env`.
- **Music:** render without baked-in music; add platform audio at upload, or music we own. No CapCut templates/sounds.
- Share the story and results, not the generation pipeline (clone risk).

## Formats (test designs; keep what wins)

**Content plan (2026-10-21):** [issue-210-content-plan.md](issue-210-content-plan.md) — series, week rhythm, reel anatomy, hooks, captions, season, metrics. It supersedes the cadence and format 6 below: founder clips are a bonus, not planned; voice = the app's voice; the monkey doesn't speak.

1. **Timer reel** — voice asks, 5 s countdown, monkey reveals the answer, one-line "why", ends with "send this to someone who'd get it wrong". 9:16, 12–20 s.
2. **Carousel** — 5–9 questions, answers on the last slide (Instagram API cap: 10 items). 1080×1350.
3. **Story** — daily question with a quiz/poll sticker (sticker added by hand, ~30 s/day).
4. **"Only N % of players knew this"** — real numbers once #205 is on in prod.
5. **Comment a topic → custom pack** — next video shows the pack being made and played (custom pack showcase).
6. **Founder plays** — screen recording of a real round + the founder answering out loud; on the personal account, best ones reposted to the brand account.

Target cadence per account: 4–5 reels/carousels per week + daily story.
