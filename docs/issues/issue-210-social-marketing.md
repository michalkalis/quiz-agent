Success after 4 weeks of posting: a weekly batch is produced and approved on one page, Instagram publishes itself, every channel has its own App Store campaign link, and we have per-language numbers (views per post, installs per channel) to decide where to put effort next.

## Hard rules

- **Driving safety:** no real footage from a moving car. Any driving visual (AI/motion) never shows a driver touching or looking at a phone — one such clip damages the whole brand and gets removed on YouTube/TikTok.
- **Only approved questions** (App Store rule: state `approved`), each with its source; the explanation line comes from the question's own explanation, not invented.
- **Real numbers only** in "X % of players knew this" posts (from #205 — daily challenge vs the crowd), never estimated.
- **Copy:** no cheesy filler, no dashes as punctuation in public text, sk/cs written natively (copy-style rules apply to captions too).
- **Credentials:** never give any tool an Instagram password or cookies; official API tokens only, in `.env`.
- **Music:** render without baked-in music; add platform audio at upload, or music we own. No CapCut templates/sounds.
- Share the story and results, not the generation pipeline (clone risk).

## Formats (test designs; keep what wins)

**Content plan (2026-10-21):** [issue-210-content-plan.md](issue-210-content-plan.md) — series, week rhythm, reel anatomy, hooks, captions, season, metrics. It supersedes the cadence and format 6 below: founder clips are a bonus, not planned; voice = the app's voice; the monkey doesn't speak.

1. **Timer reel** — voice asks, 5 s countdown, monkey reveals the answer, one-line "why", ends with "send this to someone who'd get it wrong". 9:16, 12–20 s.
2. **Carousel** — 5–9 questions, answers on the last slide (Instagram API cap: 10 items). 1080×1350.
3. **Story** — daily question with a quiz/poll sticker (sticker added by hand, ~30 s/day).
4. **"Only N % of players knew this"** — real numbers once #205 is on in prod.
5. **Comment a topic → custom pack** — next video shows the pack being made and played (custom pack showcase).
6. **Founder plays** — screen recording of a real round + the founder answering out loud; on the personal account, best ones reposted to the brand account.

Target cadence per account: 4–5 reels/carousels per week + daily story.
