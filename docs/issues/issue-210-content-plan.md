# Issue #210 — Content plan (what we post, how it is made)

**Status:** active from 2026-10-22 — posting starts as soon as the renderer exists (bios: coming soon + trubbo.app). Production uses no paid external services or models.

**Created:** 2026-10-21 · Parent: [issue-210-social-marketing.md](issue-210-social-marketing.md) · Founder-facing version: https://claude.ai/artifact/8MK4jssMpjSdkbRY8wMvNL

## Founder decisions (2026-10-21)

- **Voice:** the app's own question voice (same ElevenLabs clips as in Trubbo; generate only when missing).
- **Monkey:** visible only, no speech (founder: talking reactions too costly for now).
- **Founder clips are a bonus, not planned.** English only, when the founder has time and energy; the plan must work with zero founder clips. A founder clip replaces one agent video that week.
- **Advent quiz** (1.–24. 12.): yes, low priority, prepare later.
- Content plan accepted as the direction; platform access issues (TikTok code, personal IG) don't block the build.

## Principles

1. Every post is playable: the viewer guesses before the reveal (watch time, sends).
2. Voice is the differentiator: question read aloud. Never tell viewers to answer out loud or pause the video (founder 2026-10-23: it is Instagram, not our app; people watch however they like).
3. Series, not one-offs: numbered parts, same shape, varied visuals.
4. Vary background colour and monkey motion per category and rotate hooks: YouTube's inauthentic-content rule (2025-07) and Instagram's originality ranking (2026-04) penalise identical-looking mass output.

Stories (followers only, 24 h): published by the agent like everything else, without interactive stickers. Founder decision 2026-10-22: no quiz or poll stickers, they can only be added by hand in the Instagram app. A, B or C stays a reel, answers in the comments.

## 5 Seconds reel (9:16, ~17 s, no baked-in music)

0–1.5 s hook text · 1.5–6 s question read by the app voice, word-level captions · 6–11 s countdown, monkey thinking · 11–15 s answer + one-line why from the question's explanation · 15–17 s end card: logo, tagline ("Trivia you answer out loud" · "Kvíz, ktorý hráš hlasom" · "Kvíz, který hraješ hlasem"), coming soon + trubbo.app. No call to action (founder pick 2026-10-22). Long questions get a shortened on-screen/voice wording shown on the approval page. Test a 3-question ~45 s variant (2026 Reels data: 45–60 s gets the most views).

## Week per account

Mon 5 Seconds · Tue Road Trip Round · Wed A, B or C · Thu How it plays (or founder clip) · Fri 5 Seconds (weekend theme) · Sat/Sun off; Sunday batch approval. = 5 IG posts + 4 videos to Shorts (TikTok deferred 2026-10-23). Buffer data (2.1M posts, 2026): 3–5/wk is the sweet spot. SK/CZ account: same rhythm, one language per video, alternating sk/cs.

## Hooks (rotate; never invented percentages, no instructions to the viewer)

EN: You've got 5 seconds. · Answer before the monkey does. · One question. No googling. · Road trip round, part N.
SK: Máš 5 sekúnd. · Odpovedz skôr ako opička. · Jedna otázka, bez googlenia. · Kvíz na cestu, N. diel.
CS: Máš 5 vteřin. · Odpověz dřív než opička. · Jedna otázka, bez googlení. · Kvíz na cestu, N. díl.

## Profile bio (founder pick 2026-10-22)

EN: Odd, funny questions with a story behind the answer. You answer out loud.
SK/CZ: Vtipné a prekvapivé otázky. Pri každej odpovedi sa niečo dozvieš. Odpovedáš nahlas.
Second line on both: coming soon + trubbo.app in the link field. Never claim "no tapping" or "no screen": the app has buttons and a screen.

## Captions

Line 1 = the question (platform search). Line 2 = answer in the comments before the reveal. App named explicitly in every 3rd post, logo always. 3–5 hashtags. Link in bio → per-language page on trubbo.app with a campaign token per channel (210.7 — attribution). Clean export per platform (no other platform's watermark), caption rewritten per platform.

## Measure

Per post at 48 h: views, completion, sends, saves, comments. Weekly: profile visits, link clicks, installs per campaign link, purchases. After 8 parts a series below the account median gets a new hook or stops. Rough expectation (one weak source): ~4 installs per 1,000 views; App Store search undercounts social installs.

## Evidence (2026-10-21 pass)

- Largest quiz accounts are human head-to-head formats (Extra Medium >1M TikTok; Track Star 956K IG, 2026-02) → founder/duel clips are the strongest format when available.
- Reels length: Social Insider, ~9.8M Reels Jan–Jun 2026, median views peak at 45–60 s.
- Cadence: Buffer, 2.1M posts 2026, 3–5/wk.
- No clean data on faceless templated quiz accounts vs human; vary the template.

Sources: Social Insider reels length, Buffer posting frequency, Fox17 (Extra Medium), Paste (Track Star), PetaPixel 2026-04-30, Plagiarism Today 2025-07-08 (YouTube inauthentic content).
